UK visa application assistance

Help preparing your United Kingdom visa application, from choosing the right route to checking your documents and forms.

How we help with your UK visa application

  • Help you work out which UK visa route fits your purpose and length of stay
  • Send a document checklist matched to your route and circumstances
  • Review your documents and help complete the application forms
  • Submit your application or book your submission appointment, as the process requires
  • Keep you updated until the outcome

Choosing the right UK visa

The UK has separate visa routes for visits, study, work and family, and the documents you need differ for each. The right route depends on why you are travelling and how long you plan to stay. The UK government's online tool is the best place to confirm whether you need a visa at all. If you are unsure, tell us about your trip and we help you identify the route to look at.

What to have ready before you enquire

  • Your purpose of travel and planned dates
  • Where you plan to stay and for how long
  • Your travel history, including any earlier UK or other visa refusals
  • A summary of your work, study or business situation
  • How your trip will be funded
  • Who you are travelling with, if anyone

Please do not send passport numbers, ID numbers, bank details or document scans through the website enquiry form. After your first message we explain how to send documents.

What to expect from the process

UK visa applications are generally made online and are usually followed by an appointment at a visa application centre to provide biometric details. Appointment availability and the steps involved can change, so we confirm the current process for your application and tell you what we handle and what you need to do yourself.

What you pay

Our service fee covers our assistance with your application. UK government visa fees, any healthcare surcharge that applies to your route, and visa application centre charges are set by the UK authorities and their providers. They are separate from our fee. We confirm the amounts that apply to you before you pay.

Flight itinerary and hotel reservation

If your application needs proof of travel plans, we can supply a flight itinerary and hotel reservation as supporting documents. These are not paid tickets or confirmed stays, and we only supply genuine, verifiable reservations. Whether they are required depends on the authority handling your application.

Check the official sources

Requirements change, so always check the current rules with the official source. We confirm what applies to you before you pay.
