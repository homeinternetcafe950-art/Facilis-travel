About Facilis Travel

A private agency helping people apply for visas with less stress.

Facilis Travel helps individuals and families apply for UK, Schengen, Dubai (UAE) and China visas. We can also supply a flight itinerary and hotel reservation to support your application.

Where we are

We are based in Brits, North West, South Africa, and help clients across the country by WhatsApp, phone and email.

What we are, and what we are not

Facilis Travel is a private agency. We are not an embassy, consulate, visa centre or government office, and we are not affiliated with any of them. We assist with preparing and, where the process allows, submitting applications on your behalf. Approval decisions are made by the relevant authority, and we cannot guarantee any outcome.

How we work

We tell you which documents you need, check them with you, and confirm everything with you before anything is submitted. Read how it works, see our services or get in touch.
