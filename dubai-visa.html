Dubai (UAE) visa application assistance

Help preparing your visa application for travel to Dubai and the rest of the United Arab Emirates.

How we help with your Dubai (UAE) visa application

  • Help you confirm whether you need a visa in advance and which type applies
  • Send a document checklist matched to your trip
  • Review your documents and help complete the application forms
  • Submit your application or book your submission appointment, as the process requires
  • Keep you updated until the outcome

Which UAE visa do you need?

Whether you must apply before you travel, and which type of visa applies, depends on your passport, the purpose of your trip and how long you plan to stay. UAE entry rules have changed over time, so confirm the current position with the official UAE authority before you book. We help you work out what applies to you and what the application is likely to need.

What to have ready before you enquire

  • Your planned dates of arrival and departure
  • Where you will stay in the UAE, such as a hotel or with a host
  • Your flight plans, if you have them
  • The purpose of your trip
  • Any earlier visits to the UAE

Please do not send passport numbers, ID numbers, bank details or document scans through the website enquiry form. After your first message we explain how to send documents.

How UAE visas are processed

UAE visas are generally issued electronically through approved channels, and the right channel depends on the visa type and your circumstances. We explain which route applies to your application, what we handle for you and what you need to provide.

What you pay

Our service fee covers our assistance with your application. UAE government visa fees and any charges from the approved channel used are set by those authorities and providers, and are separate from our fee. We confirm the amounts that apply to you before you pay.

Flight itinerary and hotel reservation

If your application needs proof of travel plans, we can supply a flight itinerary and hotel reservation as supporting documents. These are not paid tickets or confirmed stays, and we only supply genuine, verifiable reservations. Whether they are required depends on the authority handling your application.

Check the official sources

Requirements change, so always check the current rules with the official source. We confirm what applies to you before you pay.

  • UAE Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP)

Facilis Travel is a private agency and is not affiliated with any government, embassy or consulate. The final decision on your application rests with the relevant authority. We cannot guarantee approval or processing times.
