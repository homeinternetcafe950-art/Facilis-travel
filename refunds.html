Refund Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Our service fee

Our service fee is refundable before work on your application starts. Once your application has been submitted, our service fee is non-refundable.

Fees paid to others

Embassy, consulate, visa-centre and other third-party fees paid on your behalf are generally non-refundable, because they are paid to and kept by those bodies.

If work has started

If work on your application has started but it has not yet been submitted, please contact us at Facilistravel@gmail.com. We will explain what applies before you decide how to proceed.

See also our Terms and the services page for what our fee covers.
