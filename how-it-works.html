How it works

Five steps from your first message to the outcome.

  1. ApplySend your details with our short form. It opens WhatsApp so you can message us.
  2. ChecklistWe send the document list for your visa.
  3. DocumentsSend your documents the secure way we show you.
  4. ConfirmWe confirm everything with you before we submit.
  5. Submit and updatesWe submit your application and keep you updated.

What happens at each step

1. Apply. Fill in the short enquiry form. It opens WhatsApp with your details ready, and nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp.

2. Checklist. We confirm the current requirements and send the document list that matches your visa and circumstances.

3. Documents. We show you how to send your documents. Please do not send passport numbers, ID numbers or bank details through the website form.

4. Confirm. We check your documents and forms and confirm everything with you, including the fees that apply, before we submit.

5. Submit and updates. We submit your application or book your submission, and keep you updated. The relevant authority makes the decision, and processing times are set by them.

Our service fee is separate from government and visa-centre fees. See our services and the Refund Policy.
