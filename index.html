What is Facilis Travel?

Facilis Travel is a private agency that helps you prepare and submit visa applications. We tell you which documents you need, check them with you, help complete the forms and keep you updated until there is an outcome.

We are independent. We are not an embassy, consulate, visa centre or government office, and we are not affiliated with any of them. The relevant authority makes every visa decision, and we cannot guarantee approval or processing times.
