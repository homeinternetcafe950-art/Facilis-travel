Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Are you affiliated with an embassy or the government?

No. We are a private agency that assists with applications. Decisions are made by the relevant embassy, consulate or authority.

Where are you based?

We are based in Brits, North West, South Africa, and help applicants across South Africa by WhatsApp, phone and email. Please contact us before planning any visit.

Which visas do you assist with?

UK, Schengen countries, Dubai (UAE) and China.

What documents do I need?

It depends on the visa and your circumstances. After you enquire, we send a checklist for your specific application.

Can you guarantee my visa will be approved?

No. The relevant authority makes the decision, and we cannot guarantee approval or processing times.

Is the flight itinerary or hotel reservation a paid booking?

No. They are supporting documents for your application, not paid tickets or confirmed stays. We only supply genuine, verifiable reservations because embassies may check. We advise waiting for your visa decision before paying for non-refundable travel.

How long does it take?

Processing times are set by the embassy or visa centre and can change. We tell you what to expect for your application and keep you updated.

What do I pay, and what is paid to others?

Our service fee covers our assistance. Government visa fees and visa-centre charges are set by the authorities and providers and are separate. We confirm the amounts that apply before you pay.

How do you handle my personal information?

We ask for only what is needed to deal with your enquiry or application, and we tell you how to send documents before you share them. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Can I get a refund?

See our Refund Policy. Embassy and visa centre fees already paid are generally not refundable.
