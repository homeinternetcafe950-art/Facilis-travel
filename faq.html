Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you apply.

Are you affiliated with an embassy or the government?

No. We are a private agency that assists with applications. Decisions are made by the relevant embassy, consulate or authority.

Which visas do you handle?

UK, all Schengen countries, Dubai (UAE) and China.

What documents do I need?

It depends on the visa. After you apply, we send a checklist for your specific application.

Is the flight itinerary or hotel reservation a paid booking?

No. They are supporting documents for your application, not paid tickets. We only supply genuine, verifiable reservations because embassies may check. We advise waiting for your visa decision before paying for non-refundable travel.

How long does it take?

Processing times are set by the embassy or visa centre and can change. We give you an estimate and keep you updated.

Is my information safe?

We collect only what is needed, handle documents carefully and delete them after your application is complete. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I get a refund?

See our Refund Policy. Embassy and visa centre fees already paid are generally not refundable.
