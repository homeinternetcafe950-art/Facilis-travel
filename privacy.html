Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

Who we are

Facilis Travel is the responsible party for personal information collected through this website and in the course of handling your enquiry or application. You can contact us at Facilistravel@gmail.com or +27 63 504 8891.

The contact page shows an embedded Google Map of the town of Brits. Loading it connects your browser to Google, which may collect technical data such as your IP address under its own privacy policy.

What we collect

Through the website forms: your name and your message or enquiry details (visa type, Schengen country, planned travel date and add-ons), and, if you choose to give them, an additional phone number and an email address.

If you go ahead with an application: we will ask for the information and documents your application needs. These can include identity and passport details, photographs and supporting documents. We tell you how to send these before you share them, and we ask that you do not send them through the website forms.

How the website forms work

The forms do not send your details to a server of ours. They open WhatsApp on your device with a message ready, and nothing is sent until you press Send in WhatsApp. Once you send it, the message is handled by WhatsApp (operated by Meta) as well as by us, under their terms and privacy policy.

Why we use it

To respond to your enquiry, prepare and submit your application, supply any flight itinerary or hotel reservation you request, and meet legal obligations.

Sharing

We share information with the relevant embassy, consulate, visa centre or approved channel as needed for your application, and with service providers who help us deliver it. We do not sell your information.

Website, cookies and analytics

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the site.

Security and retention

We limit access to the information you give us and keep it only for as long as it is needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. You can ask us what we hold about you and when it can be deleted.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction of or deletion of your personal information by emailing Facilistravel@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

See also our Terms and Refund Policy.
