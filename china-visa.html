Do you need a China visa?

China has introduced and changed visa-free and transit arrangements for different nationalities in recent years. Before you apply, check whether your passport and purpose of travel currently require a visa. If they do, the visa category, such as tourism, business or family visit, depends on why you are travelling, and the documents differ for each.

What to have ready before you enquire

  • The purpose of your trip and your planned dates
  • Your travel route and where you will stay
  • An invitation letter, where your visa category may require one
  • A summary of your work, study or business situation
  • Any earlier China visas you have held

Please do not send passport numbers, ID numbers, bank details or document scans through the website enquiry form. After your first message we explain how to send documents.

What to expect from the process

Depending on your application, you may need to attend in person at the appointed visa application service centre to provide biometric details. Requirements and procedures can change, so we confirm the current process for your application and tell you what we handle and what you need to do yourself.

What you pay

Our service fee covers our assistance with your application. Chinese visa fees and any service-centre charges are set by the relevant authorities and providers, and are separate from our fee. We confirm the amounts that apply to you before you pay.

Flight itinerary and hotel reservation

If your application needs proof of travel plans, we can supply a flight itinerary and hotel reservation as supporting documents. These are not paid tickets or confirmed stays, and we only supply genuine, verifiable reservations. Whether they are required depends on the authority handling your application.

Check the official sources
