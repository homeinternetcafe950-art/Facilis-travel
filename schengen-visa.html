Schengen visa application assistance

Help preparing a short-stay Schengen visa application for France, Germany, Italy, Spain, the Netherlands and other Schengen countries.

How we help with your Schengen visa application

  • Help you work out which Schengen country's embassy or visa centre to apply to
  • Send a document checklist matched to your destination and circumstances
  • Review your documents and help complete the application forms
  • Submit your application or book your submission appointment, as the process requires
  • Keep you updated until the outcome

Which Schengen country do you apply to?

One Schengen visa lets you travel between participating European countries, but you normally apply to the embassy, consulate or appointed visa centre of the country where you will spend the most time. If your time is split equally, it is usually the country you enter first. Each country has its own forms, checklist and appointment system, so tell us your itinerary and we help you work out where to apply.

What to have ready before you enquire

  • Your planned travel dates and a rough itinerary
  • Your main destination and any other Schengen countries you will visit
  • Where you plan to stay in each place
  • Your plan for travel insurance that meets the consulate's requirements
  • A summary of your work, study or business situation and how the trip will be funded
  • Any Schengen visas you have held before

Please do not send passport numbers, ID numbers, bank details or document scans through the website enquiry form. After your first message we explain how to send documents.

Appointments and timing

Many consulates and visa centres work by appointment, and slots can be limited in busy travel seasons. How early you may apply, and how long a decision takes, depends on the country you apply to. We tell you what we find for your application and help you book, but we cannot control appointment availability or decision times.

Flights and accommodation

Some consulates ask for proof of planned flights and accommodation. Please wait for our advice before paying for non-refundable travel. A visa decision is made by the consulate, not by us.

What you pay

Our service fee covers our assistance with your application. The Schengen visa fee, any visa-centre service charge and travel insurance are set by the consulate, its provider or the insurer, and are separate from our fee. We confirm the amounts that apply to you before you pay.

Flight itinerary and hotel reservation

If your application needs proof of travel plans, we can supply a flight itinerary and hotel reservation as supporting documents. These are not paid tickets or confirmed stays, and we only supply genuine, verifiable reservations. Whether they are required depends on the authority handling your application.

Check the official sources

Requirements change, so always check the current rules with the official source. We confirm what applies to you before you pay.
